# Humsafar — Gemini prompts for logo concepts (v1.0)

Use: start a new Gemini chat, paste the master prompt, then one concept prompt at a time. Ask for 4 variations each. Pick favourites, then use the refine and app icon prompts. The designer redraws the chosen concept in vector. Full brief: `Humsafar_Logo_Brief_v1.0.pdf`.

## Master prompt (send first)

```
You are a senior brand identity designer. I need logo concepts for a mobile app called "Humsafar". Humsafar is an Urdu word meaning "companion on a journey" or "fellow traveller". The app helps families in Pakistan look after a vulnerable loved one (an elderly parent, a chronically ill or disabled person, or someone with dementia) even when they are far away: a simple Panic button, "Are you OK?" check-ins, medicine reminders, live location with safe zones, medical records, messages and video calls, and alerts that pass from one family member to the next until someone responds. The brand must feel caring, calm, warm, trustworthy and family-centred; it must NOT feel clinical, like a hospital, alarming, or like surveillance. Main colour: teal-blue #0E7490, dark #155E75, light #ECFEFF; an optional small warm accent in amber #F59E0B. Never use red, and never use a red cross, stethoscope, pill, ECG line, eye or camera. Style: flat vector, simple geometric shapes with soft rounded corners, thick consistent strokes, no gradients, no 3D, no text inside the symbol, plain white background, centred, lots of empty space around it. It must still be recognisable as a 48-pixel app icon. Do not imitate any existing brand. Reply "Ready" and wait for my concept requests.
```

## Concept A · Two companions

```
Create 4 variations of a logo symbol for Humsafar: two simple rounded human figures side by side, a taller one gently curving an arm or arc over a smaller one, together forming a protective shape. Teal-blue #0E7490 with an optional amber #F59E0B heart or dot. Flat vector, white background, no text.
```

## Concept B · Paths that join

```
Create 4 variations of a logo symbol for Humsafar: two smooth flowing lines, like two roads or two lives, that come from different sides, meet, and continue together as one path. Where they meet they suggest a heart or the letter H. Teal-blue #0E7490 lines, small amber #F59E0B accent where the paths join. Flat vector, white background, no text.
```

## Concept C · Pin and heart

```
Create 4 variations of a logo symbol for Humsafar: a rounded map location pin whose inner space forms a heart, or holds two small simple figures close together. It should say "you always know your loved one is safe" in a warm, not technical, way. Teal-blue #0E7490 pin, amber #F59E0B heart. Flat vector, white background, no text.
```

## Concept D · Sheltering arc

```
Create 4 variations of a logo symbol for Humsafar: a soft open hand or a gentle curved arc sheltering a small figure or a small heart beneath it, showing protection and care. Keep it calm and friendly, not medical and not religious. Teal-blue #0E7490 with an amber #F59E0B centre. Flat vector, white background, no text.
```

## Concept E · H monogram

```
Create 4 variations of a monogram symbol for Humsafar based on a rounded letter H: the two vertical strokes are two simple people (small round heads on top) and the crossbar is their joined hands. One figure slightly taller than the other. Bold, simple, works as a tiny app icon. Teal-blue #0E7490, optional amber #F59E0B on the joined hands. Flat vector, white background.
```

## Concept F · Full lockup

```
Take my favourite symbol from above and show it in 3 layouts on a white background: (1) symbol left of the word "Humsafar" in a rounded bold sans-serif, dark teal #155E75; (2) symbol above the word; (3) symbol with "Humsafar" and, below it, the Urdu "ہم سفر" in Nastaliq style. Keep the text clean and correctly spelled.
```

## App icon

```
Turn my chosen Humsafar symbol into an Android app icon: a 1024 x 1024 square with rounded corners, solid teal-blue #0E7490 background and the symbol in white (with the small amber #F59E0B accent if it has one), centred and filling about 60 % of the square so it survives circular and squircle masks. Also show the same icon at small size (48 px) next to it to check it is still clear. No text.
```

## Refine

```
Refine this one: keep the idea but make the shapes simpler and the strokes thicker so it reads at 48 px. Show 4 versions: full colour on white, white on teal-blue #0E7490, one-colour dark teal #155E75, and black only. Then show it in a phone home screen next to other app icons to check it stands out. If anything looks like a hospital, an eye, a camera or uses red, remove it.
```
